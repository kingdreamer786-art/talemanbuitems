Yoga for Everyone


We are an academic teacher training facility in Somerset, KY as well as a boutique private yoga and mat pilates studio dedicated to teaching what we believe to be the essence of yoga: making a yoga practice available to everyone.


Why the Yoga and Wellness Education Centre?


take great care in preparing and equipping future yoga teachers to offer a safe and sustainable practice to their clients. We serve all walks of life, ages, shapes and experience. It is our vision to create a community of yogis coming together in unity to challenge our possibilities and uplift each other.
