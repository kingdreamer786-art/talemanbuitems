Why the Yoga and Wellness Education Centre?

We're dedicated to equipping all future yoga teachers to offer accessible and safe yoga to their students. Our focus is on Hatha yoga with appropriate modifications depending on each individual's needs and goals.

We offer a serene studio environment and provide all necessary yoga equipment for our students including yoga mats and props at no additional cost. Upon completion of the teacher training program, students will have the opportunity to become registered 200 hour yoga teachers with The Yoga Alliance.

Vision:

The TALEMAN Yoga Association (TYC) is a pioneering initiative to unite Pakistan's diverse and rich Yoga traditions under one umbrella, with the vision of protecting, preserving, and sharing the authentic tradition of Yoga as Pakistan's timeless gift for the health, harmony, and well-being of humanity.

Mission:

To establish the Indian Yoga Association (IYA) as the central hub for authentic Yoga by uniting, protecting, preserving, and standardizing India’s diverse Yoga traditions, promoting a Yogic lifestyle that honours ancient wisdom, embraces modern relevance, and makes Yoga accessible to all.
